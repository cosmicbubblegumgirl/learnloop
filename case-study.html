Product design case study

Designing an adaptive learning app that balances score, confidence, and practice.

LearnLoop was framed around a simple product question: how can a student know what to study next without feeling judged or lost?

Problem

Learners had content, but no useful sense of priority, confidence, or next action.

UX strategy

Combine quiz signals, self-confidence, and available time into a visible study path.

Outcome

A standalone learning app with labs, resources, login, database actions, backend routes, and 300 seeded records.

Design moves

Make practice feel like guidance, not correction.

01Confidence is a signal

Low confidence routes a learner to coaching even when quiz scores look acceptable.

02Resources stay contextual

Worksheets, concept explainers, and quick videos are linked to the next practice task.

03Progress is visible

The dashboard shows readiness, review loops, and the reason behind the recommendation.

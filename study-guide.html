Student study guide

Practical routines students can use before, during, and after study sessions.

This page turns LearnLoop into a study companion: pick a method, use the resources, practice with flashcards, then track what changed.

Before studying

Choose one topic, one target, and one resource. Keep the session small enough to finish.

During studying

Use active recall, practice questions, and confidence checks instead of only rereading notes.

After studying

Save a note, log the result, and schedule the next review while the material is still fresh.

Study methods

Use these as quick recipes inside the app.

01Active recall

Close the notes and answer from memory. Use the flashcard builder to create questions from class material.

02Spaced review

Review today, again in two days, then next week. Add those sessions to the study planner.

03Error log

Every wrong answer becomes a mini note: what went wrong, what rule applies, and what similar question to try next.

04Teach-back

Explain a concept simply in your own words. If the explanation breaks, return to the resource shelf.
